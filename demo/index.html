allonz-y.css Demo

A minimal, opinionated CSS system for readable, vertical content. This demo uses Merriweather for headings and intros, and Inter for body text. The background is a soft, layered radial gradient.

allonz-y.css is for anyone who wants a readable, comfortable page without fussing over a million classes or overrides. It’s just vertical rhythm, spacing, and readable line lengths—no colors, no fonts, no layout, no nonsense. Override anything you want.
